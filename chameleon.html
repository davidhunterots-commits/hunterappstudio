Countdown Chameleon

The countdown app that matches the moment.

Every big day deserves more than a plain number ticking down. Countdown Chameleon is a fully themed countdown timer app built for the moments that matter: weddings, birthdays, baby due dates, cruises, graduations, game day, Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas, and more. With 19 illustrated themes to choose from, your countdown does not just track the wait, it captures the anticipation.

Themes for Endless Occasions

From a Jolly Roger flag counting down to your beach trip to a spooky Halloween countdown, every theme is fully illustrated and built for a specific kind of moment.

Add to Calendar

Put any countdown's date and time on your iPhone calendar with one tap. The app only asks for permission to add events, so it can never read what is already in your calendar.

Keep Every Countdown Going

Track up to 30 countdowns at once, each with its own theme, swipeable like a deck of cards. Add team names, venues, cruise ships, degrees, costumes, or a baby's name to make every countdown personal.

Live, Down to the Second

Every countdown updates in real time: days, hours, minutes, and seconds, so you always know exactly how close the moment is.

Share the Excitement

Turn any countdown into a shareable image, or send a link that adds the same countdown straight into a friend's deck.

Personalize the Details

Depending on the occasion, add the details that matter: home team and opponent, school event type, gender reveal, venue, cruise line and ship, degree and field of study, costume idea, Thanksgiving dish, and more.

One Price, Everything Included

Every theme included, no ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases. Pay once, own it.

Your Data, Your Device

Countdown Chameleon works completely offline. Every countdown lives on your device, with no accounts and no tracking. Back up your deck anytime with one tap.

Also Runs on Your Mac

Countdown Chameleon also runs on Apple silicon Macs as an iPhone app, in a phone-sized window.
