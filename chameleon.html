Countdown Chameleon

The countdown app that matches the moment.

Every big day deserves more than a plain number ticking down. Countdown Chameleon is a fully themed countdown timer app built for the moments that matter — weddings, birthdays, baby's due dates, game day, graduation, and everything in between. With 14 illustrated themes to choose from, your countdown doesn't just track the wait — it captures the anticipation.

Coming soon to the Apple App Store

14 Themes, Endless Occasions

From a Jolly Roger flag counting down to your beach trip to a glowing sci-fi HUD for launch day, every theme is fully illustrated and built for a specific kind of moment — Wedding, Birthday, Baby, Football, School, Concert, and more.

Keep Every Countdown Going

Track up to 30 countdowns at once, each with its own theme, swipeable like a deck of cards. Add team names, venues, school names, or a baby's name to make every countdown feel personal.

Live, Down to the Second

Every countdown updates in real time — days, hours, minutes, and seconds — so you always know exactly how close the moment is.

Share the Excitement

Turn any countdown into a beautiful shareable image, or send a link that adds the same countdown straight into a friend's deck. Perfect for wedding parties, family group chats, or hyping up game day together.

Personalize the Details

Depending on the occasion, add the details that matter — home team and opponent, school event type, gender reveal, venue, passenger name, and more.

One Price, Everything Included

All 14 themes, no ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases. Pay once, own it.

Your Data, Your Device

Countdown Chameleon works completely offline. Every countdown lives right on your device — no accounts, no tracking. Back up your deck anytime with one tap.
