Waypoints

A travel log for your iPhone and iPad.

Every trip starts with a list: a restaurant you don't want to miss, a coffee shop a friend swore by, a hotel you're still deciding on. Waypoints keeps that scattered list in one retro-styled travel log, grouped by city and always with you. It's the place where "we should go there" turns into "we did."

Organized By City, Automatically

Add a place and Waypoints sorts it, grouped under its own postcard-style city banner, alphabetized, with a live weather check-in from Apple Weather right on the header. Scroll city by city, or view every trip at once.

A Trip for Every Adventure

Create a trip for each getaway and keep its places together. Note who recommended each stop, and filter by trip, category, status, or city, from a weekend getaway to a once-a-year international trip.

Rate It, Price It, Remember It

Mark a place Want to Go or Visited, rate it in half-stars, and set a price range from $ to $$$$. Add a photo and notes on what to order, what to skip, and what made it worth the stop.

Search Instead of Type

Look up a real restaurant, hotel, or landmark by name with Apple Maps, and Waypoints fills in the address, phone number, website, and city for you. No manual typing, no guessing at spelling.

See It All on the Map

Every place gets a map link that opens in Apple Maps, and every city gets its own map: every saved spot in that city, pinned and ready, so you can see the whole trip at a glance.

Share Any Find

Turn any place into a shareable postcard: photo, rating, price, and all, ready to drop into a post with one tap. Or send a place to a friend as a link. If they have Waypoints, it opens right in their app, ready to add to their own list.

On All Your Devices

Your list stays on your device and in your own private iCloud, so the same list shows up on your iPhone and iPad. No account to create, no ads, and no tracking.
