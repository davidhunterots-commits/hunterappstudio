Waypoints

One list, every traveler.

Every trip starts with a list: a restaurant you don't want to miss, a coffee shop a friend swore by, a hotel you're still deciding on. Waypoints turns that scattered list into one shared, living log. Built for you and whoever you're traveling with, it's the place where "we should go there" turns into "we did."

Currently in beta testing. The next trip is a great time to try it out

Organized By City, Automatically

Add a place and Waypoints sorts it, grouped under its own postcard-style city banner, alphabetized, with a live weather check-in right on the header. Scroll city by city, or view every trip at once.

Built for Every Traveler

Create a trip, add the people going, and tag who suggested each stop. Multiple trips, one shared list. Everyone sees the same places update in real time, from a weekend getaway to a once-a-year international trip.

Rate It, Price It, Remember It

Mark a place Want to Go or Visited, rate it in half-stars, and set a price range from $ to $$$$. Add notes on what to order, what to skip, and what made it worth the stop.

Search Instead of Type

Look up a real restaurant, hotel, or landmark by name and Waypoints fills in the address and city for you. No manual typing, no guessing at spelling.

See It All on the Map

Every place gets a map link, and every city gets its own live map: every saved spot in that city, pinned and ready, so you can see the whole trip at a glance.

Share Any Find

Turn any place into a shareable postcard: photo, rating, price, and all, ready to drop into a text or post with one tap.

Yours, Wherever You Are

Install it right on your phone's home screen like a real app. Every change syncs instantly between everyone on the trip, so the list is always up to date, for everyone, everywhere.
