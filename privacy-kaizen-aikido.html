Overview

Kaizen Aikido Tracker ("the App") is developed and published by Hunter App Studio ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the App.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Kaizen Aikido Tracker is designed to store all data locally on your device and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Data We Do NOT Collect

Kaizen Aikido Tracker does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email address, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash data
  • Any data to external servers or third parties

Data Stored on Your Device

All data you enter into the App — including class logs, rank progress, promotion history, techniques, seminar records, training notes, and profile information — is stored locally on your device using your device's built-in storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it using the App's built-in Backup or Share features.

User-Initiated Data Sharing

The App includes optional features that allow you to share your own data:

  • Export Backup: Downloads your training data as a JSON file to your device. You control where this file goes.
  • Share / PDF Export: Generates a progress report you can share via your device's share sheet. You choose who receives it.

We do not receive or have access to any data shared through these features.

Profile Photo

If you choose to add a profile photo, it is stored locally on your device only and is not uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

Third-Party Services

Kaizen Aikido Tracker does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected at all, the App is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
