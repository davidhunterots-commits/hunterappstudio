Gameday Countdown

Every game, right on time.

A team-colored stopwatch counts down to every kickoff, on a turf field.

Gameday Countdown turns any college football matchup into a live, team-colored stopwatch. Pick your team, and the whole screen takes on its colors while the countdown runs down to kickoff in days, hours, minutes, and seconds. Forty-eight programs come preloaded with their 2026 schedules, including the full SEC, the full Big Ten, Army, Navy, and Tulane, so a whole season is a tap away.

A Stopwatch for Kickoff

A metal stopwatch in your team's colors counts down to kickoff. The second hand ticks with the real seconds, and a ring fills as the wait goes on. It sits on a striped turf field under an end-zone band in your colors.

Your Team's Colors

The whole screen takes on your team's primary and secondary colors, with one tap to flip which one leads. Save it once and it stays that way.

Your Time Zone, Automatically

Every kickoff shows in your phone's own time zone, and the countdown and your calendar events always agree.

Always On the Next Game

At kickoff the stopwatch turns to LIVE and times the game while it is being played. Once the game is over, the countdown moves on to the next one by itself.

A List for Every Team You Follow

Keep more than one team's season going at once. Each list holds its own games, so following several teams never means losing track of any of them.

Your Lists Follow You

Sign in to iCloud and your lists, your favorite team, and your calendar records show up on your other iPhones and iPads. A new device starts with your teams already there. There is a switch on the Schedule tab if you would rather keep a device on its own.

Season Calendar and Add to Calendar

See every game from all your lists on one month view. One tap adds your team's confirmed games to your iPhone calendar. The app asks only for permission to add events, and it cannot read anything in your calendar.

Times That Keep Up

Many kickoff times are not announced until days before the game. Games without a time show their date and Time TBD. When new times come out, an Update Now banner brings them in, and any bowl or playoff games you added yourself are kept.

Setting Up a New Team's Schedule

A season takes a few minutes to build, and it only gets faster from there.

1
On the Schedule tab, tap + New List. It creates an empty schedule and drops your cursor right into the name field.
2
Type the team name (e.g. "Ohio State") to rename it.
3
On the Countdown tab, pick your team from the left side of the matchup bar (tap it to open the picker).
4
Pick the opponent, Home/Away, Game Type, and kickoff date and time in the Game Details card below.
5
Want the colors flipped from default? Tap the swap arrow, then tap Save Colors to make that stick for this team going forward.
6
Tap Save This Game. It's added to whichever schedule is currently active, the one you just created in step 1.
7
Repeat steps 3 through 6 for each game in that team's season. The same schedule keeps collecting them.
8
When you're done, use the arrows next to the big Days number (or on the Schedule tab) to swipe back to your other teams' schedules anytime.

Note: if you skip step 1 and just start adding games, they'll land in whichever schedule is already active, so creating the new list first is what keeps this team separate from your others.

How To…

Get your teams on a new device

Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple ID on both devices and open Gameday Countdown on the new one. Your lists appear within a few seconds. If they do not, close and reopen the app once.

Turn iCloud sync off or on

Open the Schedule tab and use the switch labeled Sync my lists across my devices with iCloud.

Bring in new kickoff times

Open the Schedule tab and tap Update Now when the banner appears.

Use the two-pane view

Turn an iPad sideways, or unfold an iPhone Duo and hold it wide.

Edit a saved game

Schedule tab, find the game in the list, tap Edit. It jumps you to the Countdown tab with that game's details loaded. Change anything, then tap Save Changes to update it in place, or Cancel to back out.

Switch between schedules

Use the arrow buttons next to the big Days number, or swipe left and right anywhere on the scoreboard. The same arrows also appear at the top of the Season Schedule panel on the Schedule tab.

See your whole season

Tap the Calendar tab, use the arrows to change months, and tap a day with dots to see its games.

Remove a schedule

Schedule tab, switch to the schedule you want to remove, tap Remove This List. You'll be asked to confirm, since this deletes every game saved in it. Disabled if it's your only schedule.

Remove a single game

Schedule tab, find the game in the list, tap Remove.

Turn auto-advance on or off

Schedule tab, the "Auto-advance to the next upcoming game" checkbox. When it's on, the countdown always shows whichever saved game is soonest and moves itself forward once a game passes. Turn it off to browse and hold on one game manually.

Change a team's colors for good

Tap the swap arrow next to the color swatches to preview the flip, then tap Save Colors. This updates the default for that team going forward and updates every game you've already saved for that team, so the change actually sticks everywhere.

Move a list by file

Schedule tab, tap Download Schedule, then choose Save to Files in the share sheet (or AirDrop or send it). On another device, tap Upload Schedule and pick that file. It replaces whatever schedule is active there, so switch to (or create) the right list first. This works without iCloud.

Share the game on screen

Countdown tab, Copy Link. This copies a web address with that exact matchup baked in: team, colors, opponent, and kickoff time, so whoever opens it sees the same countdown you do.

Add a non-conference or out-of-list opponent

The opponent field on the Countdown tab is a plain text box. Just type any name, it doesn't have to be one of the teams in the dropdown.

Add games to your calendar

Schedule tab, tap Add to Calendar, allow Add Events Only the first time, and the games appear in the Calendar app. If a kickoff time changes after you've added a game, tap again to add the new time and delete the old event.

Set a favorite team

Schedule tab, tap the star next to the schedule's name.

Whether you are locked in on one team or tracking a handful, Gameday Countdown keeps you counting down to what matters: kickoff.
